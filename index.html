About

Zabibu Composer

A visual instrument for drawing music — make a loop, load an African groove, and export something finished.

Designed and built by Tehillah Kachila in Nairobi, Kenya. Zabibu Composer is a Zabibu Technika product.

The Zabibu Technika Ring Tune was made with Zabibu Composer. .

Original code, sounds, and drawings. Clean-room implementation inspired by the visual-instrument idea; not affiliated with play_music_theory.

Everyone can make music

Accessibility

Keyboard composer

Tab to the canvas. Arrow keys move the cursor, Enter adds a note, Delete removes it, Space plays or stops, and [ / ] change the note length. Press 1–9 to choose an instrument.

Voice commands

Say “play”, “stop”, “add note”, “erase”, “next instrument”, “surprise me”, or “export MP3”.

First time?

Draw a line. Hear a melody.

  1. Pick an instrument. Each color is a different sound.
  2. Draw on the canvas. Left to right is time; up and down is pitch.
  3. Press play. The loop repeats. Add more layers with 1, 2, and 3.
  4. Export. WAV, MP3, MP4, MIDI, or a 30-second ringtone — mixed and mastered for you.
